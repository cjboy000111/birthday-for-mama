Chapter One · Even from far away

Some miles can separate us,
but never my love for you.

Mom, I wish I could be there with you today. I wish I could see your smile, hear your voice beside me, and celebrate this special day with you in person.

But even when we are far apart, you are still part of my everyday life. There are moments when I wish you were closer, when I wish I could tell you something in person instead of through a screen.

"I may not always be beside you, but you are never far from my heart."

And if there is one thing I want you to remember today, it is this: distance may change how often we see each other, but it will never change the fact that you are my mom.

Chapter Two · Words from my heart

Things I don't always say.

Sometimes the most important words are the ones we forget to say. So today, I want to say a few of them.

01

Thank you, Mom.

Thank you for everything you have done for me, including the things I may not have fully understood when I was younger. I may not always express it well, but I am grateful to have you in my life.

02

I miss you.

I miss the simple things that we cannot always share when we are apart. Sometimes, no special occasion is needed to make me wish you were closer. I just miss having my mom near me.

03

I'm still learning.

I know I am not perfect. I still have so much to learn, and I know there are times when I could have done better. But I hope you know that I am trying to grow into someone who can face life with courage and kindness.

04

I love you, always.

Not only on your birthday. Not only when everything is going well. Even on ordinary days, even when life gets busy, you will always have a place in my heart that no distance can take away.

Chapter Three · A letter just for you

If I could be there,
this is what I'd tell you.

I cannot put all my feelings into a single letter. But I hope these words help you understand what you mean to me.

Dear Mom,

Please read this slowly. I wrote it with love.

Happy birthday, Mom. ❤️

I wish I could be there with you today. I wish I could celebrate beside you, see your smile, and give you a birthday hug. But since I cannot be there in person, I wanted to make something that comes straight from my heart.

Mom, I know that as I grow older, life takes me through different places and experiences. Sometimes, I get busy trying to figure things out. Sometimes, I forget to say the things that matter most.

But no matter how much life changes, I never want you to think that being far away means I love you any less. I still think about you. I still wish you were closer on days when I need you. And I still wish I could share more of my life with you in person.

Thank you for being my mom. Thank you for the things you have taught me, the care you have given me, and the ways you have tried to help me through life. I may not understand every sacrifice, but I know that your place in my life is something I will always treasure.

I am sorry for the times I may not have shown enough appreciation. I am still learning how to be a better son, and I know I have room to grow. I hope that as time goes on, I become better at showing you how much I care.

For your birthday, I wish you more than gifts or a happy day. I wish you good health, peace in your heart, and the kind of happiness that stays even after a celebration ends. I hope you have time to rest, to laugh, to follow your own dreams, and to enjoy all the beautiful things life still has waiting for you.

I also hope that one day, I can give back some of the love and kindness I have received. I want to keep growing, keep working toward my dreams, and build a life that makes me proud of who I become.

I cannot promise that everything will be easy. I cannot promise that I will never make mistakes. But I can promise to keep learning, to keep trying, and to never forget how much you mean to me.

If there is one thing I wish I could tell you while sitting right beside you today, it is that I miss you. I wish we had more time together. I wish distance were not part of our story. But until the day we can share more moments in person, I hope you can feel a little of my love through these words.

Happy birthday, Mom. I hope this new year of your life brings you gentle days, beautiful surprises, and countless reasons to smile.

Wherever life takes me, I will always be grateful that I get to call you my mom.

I love you, always. ♡

With all my love,
Your son.
